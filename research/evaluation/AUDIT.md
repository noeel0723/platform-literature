- LiteratureIdentityNormalizer uses Unicode title normalization and validates ISBN-10/13 checksums. Invalid numeric-looking identifiers become source-scoped catalog identifiers. Source external IDs and knowledge-graph IDs are also identifiers. It does not implement broad semantic embeddings/fuzzy title matching.

LiteratureSourceMapping has unique literature_id and provider/external ID, a canonical foreign key, status/method/confidence/provenance. CanonicalWorkIdentifier has unique scheme/value. Author aliases, author identity normalization and pivot position govern primary authors. Those upstream author identities are frozen inputs; they are not human labels.
